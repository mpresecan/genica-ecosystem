# Genica — product truth

Four commercial properties operated by one studio. Every site's job is the
same: get a qualified person onto a call.

## The operator

Fortbit d.o.o., a Croatian-registered software studio operating from Poland.
Founder-led, senior people brought in per engagement. EU invoicing,
reverse-charge VAT for EU business clients. No equity taken, ever.

## The four lines

| Line | Domain | Sells | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Genica | genica.org | SaaS product engineering, MVP→MLP, fractional technical direction | Founders, pre-seed to Series A; operators building outside the roadmap |
| Aigenica | aigenica.com | Consulting: installing an agentic delivery pipeline | Engineering leaders whose agent pilot will not survive a rollout |
| Edugenica | edugenica.com | Branded course / conference / event platforms | Consultants, trainers, experts, event organisers |
| Flowgenica | flowgenica.com | Agency delivery workflow — consulting now, SaaS in build | Agency and studio owners past ~8 people |

## Proof we are allowed to claim

- Layron, an AI deal workspace built for **Layer Finance**, in production.
- **80% time saved during deal onboarding**, measured and stated by the client.
- Quotes from Kurt Praszl (Layer Finance) and Tim-Julian Hartmann (Peakside
  Projects), verbatim from the previous genica.org.
- `payload.solutions` — the studio's open-source Payload CMS work: the Payload
  App Stack, Payload Clock, and five plugins. MIT licensed, publicly readable.

Nothing else is a claim. Aigenica and Edugenica have no named clients on the
sites. Flowgenica states in the first viewport that the software is in build.

## Conversion model

**One action across all four sites: book a call.** A single `BOOKING_URL` in
`packages/ui/src/brand/index.ts`. Email appears as a quiet fallback, never as a
competing button. No contact forms, no newsletter, no gated PDFs.

Every call promise is specific and keepable — "45 minutes; you leave with a
scope, a sequence and a number, whether or not you hire us."

## Constraints

- Never name the vendor stack behind Aigenica's pipeline work. The offer is
  the consulting, and the pipeline is deliberately vendor-neutral.
- No CMS. Content is TypeScript in each app.
- No invented prices, clients, metrics, launch dates or testimonials.
