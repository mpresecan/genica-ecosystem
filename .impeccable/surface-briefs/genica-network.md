# Surface brief — the Genica network (all four properties)

Scope: genica.org, aigenica.com, edugenica.com, flowgenica.com — every route.
Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The visitor decides whether to book a call.

Audience: a founder or agency owner at a laptop mid-morning with three vendor
tabs open, about to forward one link to a co-founder. Action: book a call.
Proof available: Layron for Layer Finance, 80% measured onboarding saving, two
named quotes, and the open-source Payload work. Constraint: no invented
clients, prices, metrics or dates; never name the vendor stack behind Aigenica.

Direction: **Quiet** — the category standard, taken deliberately as the
standing exit after a full direction round (lead was Terms of Engagement;
Imprint and Programme competitive; Certificate and matte dark declined).
Named craft bar, set by the client: **Linear, Stripe, Vercel**. Their level of
spacing discipline, optical detail and restraint is the bar this must reach.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** A studio whose argument is that it decides before it builds, on a
page that behaves the same way: nothing arrives that was not decided. It
refuses the arrangement the category reaches for when it wants to look
serious — heavy expanded display type, uppercase tracked labels, filled
brand-colour buttons, a dark panel beside the headline, texture as proof of
rigour.

**OWN-WORLD.** Cool near-white #FBFBFC on one surface throughout, with a
single recessed #F3F3F5 band. One grotesque — Host Grotesk — at 380 body and
420 display, never above 520. Hairlines are the only structure: no cards, no
fills, no shadows, no radius except 8px on buttons. Colour appears three times
per page at most: a 5px dot, a link underline on hover, and the network marks.
Recognisable with all content removed by the hairline grid and the absence of
every device the category uses.

**STORY.** He understands within one screen that this studio writes a
specification before it writes code; he believes it because the page itself is
evidence of the same discipline; he books the call because one action is
offered and the promise attached to it is specific enough to be falsifiable.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Single column, left-aligned, generous. Headline at 3rem
max, weight 420, tracking −0.03em, four lines at most. One paragraph at 52ch.
An ink button with a 28ch promise beside it. Beneath, three hairline rows —
Scope, Ownership, Basis — carrying the facts a buyer checks first. Nothing
else above the fold: no panel, no illustration, no metric tile.

**FORM.** The category canon, played straight, taken as the standing exit on
the client's explicit instruction after the direction round. It topped my own
grounded ranking as the Quiet card. No seed key — `concept-seed` could not
run; impeccable.style is unreachable from both machines, disclosed to the
client and recorded here.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with
the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying
its provenance
